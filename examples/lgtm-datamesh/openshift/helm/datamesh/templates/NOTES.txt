The data mesh is deploying to namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

Watch it come up:
  oc get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -w

Infrastructure (postgres, kafka, apicurio) starts first; the seven data-product
services become Ready once their probes pass. The external entry points are
Routes:
  graphql-gateway:  oc get route graphql-gateway -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.spec.host}'
  apicurio:         oc get route apicurio -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.spec.host}'

The graphql-gateway Route is the system's query edge; order-service publishes
`order-placed` events to Kafka, which notification-service consumes (Avro,
schema fetched from Apicurio). Everything else is ClusterIP-only (reachable
in-cluster).

Out of scope for this chart (document-only in the tutorial's appendix, not
deployed here): OpenMetadata, Prefect, the LGTM observability stack, KEDA
autoscaling, and the Istio mesh.
